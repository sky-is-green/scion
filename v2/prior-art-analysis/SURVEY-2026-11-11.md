# Scion v2 research sweep - 2026-11-11

Broad scan since the last pass (2026-08 onward): arXiv, Hugging Face, web.
Everything here is a candidate; nothing is adopted. Sources are listed with
ids/dates so we can fetch full text on demand.

## 1. Headline signals for v2

1. Our loop finding is not unique. "Right On Q" (Oct 21) documents exactly
   this: quantized reasoning models "often fail to terminate within a token
   budget that suffices for the full-precision model" on long-horizon tasks.
   Long-horizon non-termination is a recognized quantization failure mode, and
   the paper's fix is QAT on self-generated trajectories.
2. 2-bit work is moving from PPL to reasoning. "ThinkBit" (Oct 26) argues
   PPL/MCQ say little about reasoning and builds a 2-bit framework judged on
   reasoning. This validates the hard-90 protocol over KLD/PPL.
3. The community is using our container names. Ternary-Flint-40B ships
   PTQ1_0 (Type 143) and PQ2_0 (Type 142) quants in 9.3-12.5 GB, with
   Hadamard-H512 folding, latent-STE KD (top-64 logits), and a scale
   attenuation correction. They do calibration/KD, not trained corrections.
4. A 30B-A3B ternary competitor exists: Nota AI ternarized Nemotron 3.5
   Lightning (60 GB -> 9.6 GB) and recovers ~89% of the teacher's benchmark
   average; a lossless GGUF repack runs at 8.82 GB, 368K context, ~172 tok/s
   on a 12 GB card.
5. Embeddings are a real size lever for us: "Embeddings Are Overpriced?"
   (Oct 15) shows the vocab table dominates small/ternary files because it
   stays 16-bit. Our 35B ships Q8_0 embeddings + head; that is ~1.5-2 GB of
   the 11.3 GB.
6. Export can silently undo ternary work: "Lost in the bf16 Cast" (Oct 6)
   finds shipped ternary checkpoints disagree with their deployed codes on
   0.8-1.5% of weights because of bf16 rounding at the export step.

## 2. Must-read papers (v2-relevant)

### Reasoning under quantization
- **Right On Q: Recovering Reasoning under Quantization via Self-Generated
  Trajectories** - 2610.29378, 2026-10-21. Non-termination within budget on
  long-horizon reasoning; existing QAT supervises fixed responses and misses
  the student's own failure states. QAT on self-generated trajectories.
- **ThinkBit: Search Codewords, Recalibrate Scales, Select by Reasoning** -
  2610.34886, 2026-10-26. 2-bit framework built to preserve reasoning;
  codeword search + reasoning-based selection; argues PPL/MCQ are the wrong
  metrics.
- **SteerDistill: Quantizing LLM Agents via Closed-Loop Teacher Steering** -
  2611.13410, 2026-11-10. Quantized agents overlook task requirements and
  environment conditions; corrects behavioral divergence on failed rollouts
  with a teacher in the loop. Directly relevant to our tools family.
- **Quantization-Aware Healing: A Practical Recipe for Recovering Compressed
  4-Bit LLMs** - 2608.20953, 2026-08-21. QAT converged slowly and collapsed
  past its peak; a healing recipe instead.
- **ResQAdam: Residual-Preserving Adaptive Optimization for QAT** -
  2611.08924, 2026-11-06. AdamW's FP32 states are the memory wall; low-rank
  optimizers (GaLore/LDAdam) degrade in QAT; keeps the residual sign.

### Ternary conversion and scales
- **RoleQ: Role-Aligned Scales for Converting Language Models to Ternary** -
  2610.21808, 2026-10-15. Scales by the functional role of transformer units
  (residual readers vs writers) instead of matrix layout; ternary without
  long fine-tuning. The closest published idea to our role_map.
- **Breaking the 1.58-bit Barrier for Ternary LLMs** - 2609.16338,
  2026-09-14. Five-trit packing rounds to 1.625 effective bits; measures the
  real storage/quality trade.
- **SQuAT: Spherical Lattice Vector Quantization-Aware Training** -
  2610.31412, 2026-10-22.
- **SGA-Sparse: Training Native SwiGLU Gates for Activation-Sparse and
  Low-Bit LLMs** - 2611.11223, 2026-11-08. Gate/up ordering flips after
  sparsity-aware training; relevant if we ever add activation sparsity.

### Ultra-low-bit MoE
- **ProQuant: Preserving Expert Quality over Quantity in Ultra-Low-Bit
  Quantization of Mixture-of-Experts Models** - 2610.30779, 2026-10-22.
  Mixed-precision PTQ for MoE under tight budgets; cross-layer propagation of
  quantization error.
- **MatMoE: Matryoshka Mixture-of-Experts with Dynamic Mixed-Precision
  Quantization** - 2611.01704, 2026-11-01. Per-token dynamic precision.
- **Turing LLM Post-Training: Calibrated Distillation and Efficient
  Dynamic-MoE Execution** - 2610.39876, 2026-10-29. Calibrated multi-teacher
  on-policy distillation with top-K support matching.
- **Nemotron 3.5 Super and Lightning** - 2610.38095, 2026-10-28. 30B-A3B
  Lightning; large-scale distillation, long-context, native speculative
  decoding. NVIDIA's QAD pipeline reports 99.24% of BF16 at NVFP4, and the
  MTP module is not quantized.

### Compensation and correction
- **Align, Then Correct: Training-Free Two-Stage Low-Rank Compensation for
  Extremely Quantized LLMs** - 2610.08164, 2026-10-06. Closed-form rank-r
  adapters beside frozen quantized weights; symmetric calibration makes the
  target high-rank. A training-free alternative to our trained corrections.
- **Quantization of Large Models with Weight Outliers and Sparsity** -
  2611.00286, 2026-10-30. Decompose into quantized matrix + low-rank +
  sparse outlier component.
- **When Does Compensating Inherited Quantization Error Help?** - 2610.16020,
  2026-10-12. Mean-square guarantees and limits for layer-wise compensation.
- **QATFactory: A Versatile, Deployment-Aligned Framework for QAT and
  Distillation of LLMs** - 2609.39223, 2026-09-30. Simulates deployment
  quantization while computing in BF16; QAD/QARL.

### Size, export, formats
- **Embeddings Are Overpriced? A Frozen 16 MB Vocabulary for Small and
  Ternary Language Models** - 2610.22428, 2026-10-15. Replace the embedding
  table with a frozen 512-bit-code table; the table is most of the file.
- **Lost in the bf16 Cast: Exporting Ternary Language Models Can Revert Most
  Low-Learning-Rate Code Changes** - 2610.07853, 2026-10-06.
- **BitMTP: When Multi-Token Prediction Meets Low-Bit LLMs** - 2610.28500,
  2026-10-20. MTP + ultra-low-bit backbone; direct combination does not
  accelerate at comparable quality.
- **Few Bits, One Law: Toward W2A4KV2** - 2610.09202, 2026-10-06. Unified
  weight/activation/KV quantization with canonicalization + codebooks.
- **SHq: Learnable Grids for LLM Weight Quantization** - 2611.02966,
  2026-11-02. No fixed grid is universally optimal.
- **Trainable Vector Quantization for LLMs** - 2610.41822, 2026-10-30. STE
  training for VQ codebooks.

### Long chain-of-thought behavior
- **Think Less, Locally: Local Preference Learning for Reasoning
  Compression** - 2611.04814, 2026-11-03. Models keep deliberating after
  enough information; local stop/continue preference.
- **Is Token Confidence Just Chain Length? A Signal-Processing Audit of
  Reasoning Traces** - 2610.36709, 2026-10-27. Confidence-based chain
  selection is confounded by length; relevant to our loop metric and
  budget policy.

## 4. What this changes or confirms for v2

- Confirmed: long-horizon non-termination is a known low-bit failure and the
  right metric. Keep the hard-90 and the loop-onset metric.
- Confirmed: corrections-only is viable, but the literature now has two
  competing routes worth benchmarking against our plan:
  a) trained corrections with the quantizer in the loop (ours), and
  b) training-free low-rank compensation (Align, Then Correct).
- New lever: the embedding/head. A frozen/binarized vocabulary could take
  ~1.5-2 GB off the 11.3 GB tier without touching the expert body.
- New audit: reproduce "Lost in the bf16 Cast" on our export path; if true,
  some of our published ternary code may not match the intended latents.
- New idea for corrections data: SteerDistill's closed-loop steering on
  failed rollouts maps directly onto our tools failures and onto the hard-90
  coding truncations.
- Rotation is not dead: Flint combines Hadamard-512 with KD and reports
  gains. Our D1 only tested rotation before RTN without a KD stage. Worth a
  controlled retest (rotation + KD + corrections).
- Competitive bar: a 30B-A3B ternary at 8.82 GB with 368K context and ~172
  tok/s exists. Scion 35B at 11.3 GB must beat its long-horizon behavior,
  not just its size.
